Only the paper's authors do this. You need: a published paper with a DOI or an arXiv ID · an AI assistant (an agent that runs commands suits any paper; a chat assistant, only a short one) · an ORCID iD · time to check 20 to 40 claims.

Your assistant cannot open web pages? Download the full message (about 15,000 tokens) and attach it with your paper instead of the line.

How it goes

Your assistant does the writing. You check it and answer for it.

  1. YouAttachyour paper, with the line
  2. AssistantDraftthe claims, each with where it comes from
  3. YouAnswerthat you are an author, the licence, what matters most
  4. YouCheckevery claim against your paper
  5. AssistantSubmitafter checking the file
  6. YouConfirmwith ORCID, within 48 hours

Before you confirm, see what the responsible researcher checks.

Why it is worth it

Found where research is searchedListed for search engines and answer engines, and open to any assistant through EntryLib's MCP server.
