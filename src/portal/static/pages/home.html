EntryLib, an open library of research papers written for people and AI

Research papers you can check claim by claim

EntryLib publishes research in a form that both people and AI can read and check. Instead of a long PDF, each paper is broken down into its claims, one finding each. Every claim shows what it is based on (the data, the proof or the experiment) along with what it assumes and where it may not hold, so you can see quickly what a paper really shows and how solid it is. A named researcher vouches for every paper, and it is free to read, search and publish.

Read it yourself

  1. Pick a paper below, or search.
  2. Read its Summary and Highlights.
  3. Open any claim to see the evidence it rests on.
  4. Each claim has a Support score from 0 to 100 (how it is given).

Ask your AI

  1. On a paper's page, press Ask your AI about this paper.
  2. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and ask what you like.
