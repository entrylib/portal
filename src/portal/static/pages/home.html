The open library of machine-readable entries

EntryLib publishes research in a form that both people and AI can read and check. Instead of a long PDF, each paper is broken down into its findings, one by one. Every finding shows what it is based on (the data, the proof or the experiment) along with what it assumes and where it may not hold, so you can see quickly what a study really shows and how solid it is. A named researcher vouches for every entry, and it is free to read, search and publish.
