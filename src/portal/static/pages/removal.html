Retraction and removal

A published version is never changed. When something is wrong with an entry, it is corrected, marked, retracted or, for legal reasons, removed.

Correcting and disputing

An error is fixed by a new version, which the guarantor submits and confirms; earlier versions stay readable and say that a newer one exists. Disagreement with an entry is not a reason to remove it: publish a claimset that disputes it, or write to us if you think it needs a notice.

Retraction

A retracted entry stays readable, with a notice at its top that says who retracted it, when, and why.

Removal

An entry is removed only when it must be: it infringes someone's rights, it holds personal data that should not be public, or it is unlawful. Its content is replaced at the same address by a notice that says who removed it, when, and why; its identifiers keep resolving to that notice.

How to ask

Write to mail@entrylib.org with the entry's identifier, what you ask for (retraction or removal), and why. The guarantor's request is acted on once we have confirmed it is theirs; anyone else's is reviewed. A form, with sign-in by ORCID so that a guarantor can act at once, is coming.
