Before you submit

Your agent can write and submit a claimset, but you publish it: as its guarantor, the responsible researcher, you answer for every claim in it. Go through this list before you confirm.

1. You are the guarantor

The guarantor is one person with an ORCID iD, named in the file, who answers for the research and is the person to contact. After your agent submits, you get a confirmation link: sign in with that ORCID iD within 48 hours, or the submission is dropped. Nobody can publish in your name without signing in as you.

2. Read every claim against your results

Check that each claim says what your results show, under the conditions they were obtained, and nothing more. Look for numbers that differ from your tables, conditions dropped from a statement, and conclusions broader than the evidence. Results where your method did worse belong in the claims too. Remove anything your agent inferred that you cannot stand behind.

3. Check the evidence and its material

Each claim should point to evidence that shows it. Links to data, code and other material should work and be public, or say why they are not. Do not include personal or confidential data.

4. Validate, read and score

Have your agent run claimset validate and fix every error, then read the reading view (claimset render) yourself from start to finish. Optionally, ask it to run the score tool of the EntryLib connector: it shows the Support scores the claimset would get and its biggest issues, before anything is published.

5. Choose the licence

The licence, an SPDX identifier such as CC-BY-4.0, applies to the published file for good: every version is kept in the public registry.

6. Know what publishing means

  • A published version is never changed. A correction is a new version, submitted the same way; the page shows that the paper was updated and keeps every version.
  • Every submission is checked against the specification and screened for text aimed at manipulating AI readers. A submission that fails is refused with the reasons, and nothing is kept.
  • Each guarantor can publish up to {new_per_week} new claimsets in any seven days, and each claimset up to {versions_per_day} new versions a day.
  • Retraction and removal follow a published policy.
  • Submit your own research only: a paper you have already published is welcome (see Turn your paper into a claimset); papers by others are not submitted here.

To see what a claimset looks like, open the example claimset file, or any paper here: its page links its file under "For models". To make one from a paper you have written, see Turn your paper into a claimset.
