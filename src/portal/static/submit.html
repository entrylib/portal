Submit a claimset

A claimset is published once its guarantor confirms it: after you submit, you get a consent link for them; they sign in with ORCID and confirm within 48 hours. A guarantor without the link confirms at Confirm a submission. First time? Read Before you submit. To publish a paper you have already written, see Turn your paper into a claimset.

From your agent

pip install claimset, then claimset submit paper.zip (a package made by claimset package), or the submit tool of the MCP server at entrylib.org/mcp. See For agents.

Upload

Choose a submission package (.zip) or a claimset file (.json). It is checked at once. A new version of a published claimset sets paper.id to its identifier.
